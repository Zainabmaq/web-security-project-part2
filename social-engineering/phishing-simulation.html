You just fell for a phishing attack!

This was a simulated phishing exercise conducted as part of our cybersecurity awareness training. No real data was collected or stored.

The fake login page you just saw was designed to look like Facebook. Real attackers use similar techniques to steal your credentials.

🚩 Red flags you should have noticed:

  • The URL was NOT facebook.com
  • No HTTPS padlock in the address bar
  • You received this link via email/message unexpectedly
  • The page asked for login without a clear reason

✅ How to Stay Safe:

  • Always check the URL before entering credentials
  • Never click links in suspicious emails
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
  • Use a password manager
  • Report suspicious emails to your IT team
